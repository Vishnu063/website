# Yoru business plan

## Available now

Create Persona: original anime vector customizer, a short creative quiz, saved looks on each device, exact-look sharing links, and matching PNG card/profile/banner exports. Existing Yoru discovery pages remain available.

## Public launch

The current Sites deployment is owner-private. External visitors and shared links require a public hosting decision before a growth campaign. For DevOps practice, another route is deploying the Docker/nginx image to a user-controlled host and domain.

## Validate usefulness

Invite a first small audience after public launch. Measure completed creations, successful downloads, voluntary sharing and return visits. Add analytics only after choosing a provider and reviewing its data collection. No revenue or traffic is guaranteed.

## Potential revenue experiments

1. Artist-created outfit and background collections with clear usage rights.
2. Optional premium social identity kits with additional export formats.
3. Clearly labelled direct sponsorships on editorial/discovery pages.
4. An ad-network application after the site has an audience and the current provider's eligibility/content requirements have been reviewed.

## Advertising placement

Keep the creator canvas, controls, quiz answers and download buttons free of ads. Consider a clearly labelled sponsor position after editorial content or below completed creations. Avoid pop-ups, download-looking ads, forced ad clicks and artificial engagement. Network approval, income and eligibility are not established by this plan. No ad scripts or tracking have been installed.

## Later product work

Commission richer modular anime illustrations, add cross-device accounts/cloud saves, test actual weekly content releases, and consider moderation before any public submissions. Choose these from visitor feedback. Paid AI generation would need a selected provider, budget and abuse controls; do not present static presets as AI-generated visitor requests.
